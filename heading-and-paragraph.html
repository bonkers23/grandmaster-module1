Heading and Paragraph in HTML

Headings and paragraphs are used to display text content in a structured and readable format.

HTML Headings

HTML provides six heading tags from h1 to h6. h1 is the largest heading and h6 is the smallest.

HTML Paragraph

The paragraph tag is used to write text content. Each paragraph starts on a new line.
